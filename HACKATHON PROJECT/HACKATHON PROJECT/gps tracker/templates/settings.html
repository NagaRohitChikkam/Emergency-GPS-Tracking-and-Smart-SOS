📧 Gmail Email Configuration

How to get a Gmail App Password (required):

  1. Go to your Google Account
  2. Click Security → enable 2-Step Verification first
  3. Then go to Security → App Passwords
  4. Choose app: Mail, device: Other, name it "GPS Tracker"
  5. Copy the 16-character password shown (no spaces)
  6. Paste it in the App Password field below
→ Open Google App Passwords Page
⚠️ This is your App Password, NOT your regular Gmail login password.

How to set up Twilio (free trial — no credit card needed):

  1. Go to twilio.com/try-twilio and create a free account
  2. Verify your email and phone number
  3. On the Twilio Console, copy your Account SID and Auth Token
  4. Click "Get a free phone number" → copy the number (e.g. +12345678900)
  5. With a trial account, you can only call verified numbers — add contacts' numbers at Twilio Verified Caller IDs
  6. Paste all three values below and click Save
